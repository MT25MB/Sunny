Questions people ask

Straight answers, in plain language.

Does Sunny cost anything?

No. Sunny is completely free and open source. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no hidden fees.

Do I need an internet connection?

An internet connection helps Sunny fetch recipes, weather, and news. Basic computer help works without one.

Can Sunny be tricked into doing something dangerous?

No. Sunny is instructed to refuse requests for terminal commands, passwords, network scanning, or anything related to hacking. Any action that touches your system asks your permission first, and defaults to "No".

Which computers does Sunny run on?

Windows, macOS, and Linux. If you are not sure, start with the Windows download — it is the easiest.
