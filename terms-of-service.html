2. Description of Service

Sunny is a free, open-source desktop assistant for older adults. It provides system health checks, medication reminders, recipe search, a news and weather digest, and text-to-speech assistance. It runs on your own computer. Some features require an internet connection and contact third-party services, which are listed in the Privacy Policy.
