# Sunny Voice Guide

## We Are
- **Warm but not saccharine** — like a helpful neighbor, not a greeting card
- **Clear but not condescending** — explain like you're teaching your grandma, not a child
- **Confident but not boastful** — we know it works, we don't need to shout
- **Practical, not poetic** — every word earns its keep

## We Sound Like
- "Sunny helps with your computer, medications, meals, and day."
- "No accounts. No tracking. Just download and go."
- "Speak or type. Sunny listens."
- "Built for older adults. Tested by real ones."

## We DON'T Sound Like
- "Sunny is a gentle AI desktop companion..." (generic AI marketing)
- "Seamlessly integrate..." (corporate speak)
- "Unlock the power of..." (hype)
- "Elevate your experience..." (empty verb)

## Sentence-Level Rules
- **No em dashes ever** — use commas, periods, or separate sentences
- **No semicolons** — they read academic
- **Contractions encouraged** — "you'll" not "you will", "it's" not "it is"
- **Short sentences** — aim for 12-18 words average
- **Active voice** — "Sunny reminds you" not "You are reminded by Sunny"
- **Specific over abstract** — "reminds you to take your pill" not "provides medication adherence support"

## Forbidden Words List
delve, tapestry, realm, elevate, unlock, seamless, robust, leverage, synergy, paradigm, holistic, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, best-in-class, innovative, solution, empower, transform, journey, landscape, ecosystem, harness, maximize, optimize, streamline, facilitate, enable, utilize

## Em Dash Replacements
| Original | Rewrite |
|----------|---------|
| "Sunny—your helper—does X" | "Sunny helps with X." |
| "Free—open source—no tracking" | "Free. Open source. No tracking." |
| "It works—really well—every time" | "It works well. Every time." |

## Context Tone Shifts
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Landing page hero | Warm, confident | "Technology help that feels like a sunny day" |
| Feature descriptions | Practical, specific | "Set a pill name and time. Sunny speaks up when it's time." |
| FAQ | Direct, honest | "No. Sunny is completely free." |
| Error messages | Empathetic, actionable | "Something went wrong. Try restarting Sunny." |
| Settings/prefs | Clear, instructive | "Choose how loud Sunny speaks." |
| README (dev audience) | Technical but accessible | "Run `npm run dev` to start the development server." |
