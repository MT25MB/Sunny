Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

1. Who We Are

Sunny is a free, open-source desktop companion built for older adults. Sunny is developed as a community project and is available on GitHub at github.com/MT25MB/Sunny.

2. Local-First Design

Sunny is designed to run locally on your computer. By default, all processing happens on your machine. Nothing you type or say is sent to a remote server unless you explicitly enable an optional cloud feature.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information: We do not collect your name, email, address, or any identifying information.
  • No accounts: Sunny does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • No tracking: We do not install cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics on your computer.
  • No advertising: Sunny shows no ads and shares no data with advertisers.
  • No data selling: We do not sell, rent, or license your data to anyone, ever.

4. Optional Cloud Features

Some features (such as recipe search via DuckDuckGo or daily news) may use public, free web endpoints. When you use these features:

  • Only the specific search query you type is sent to the public service.
  • No personal identifiers are attached to the request.
  • You can disable these features at any time in Settings.

Sunny can also use local AI inference via Ollama when available. Ollama runs entirely on your computer — no prompts or responses leave your device.

5. Children Privacy

Sunny is designed for users of all ages, including older adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

Because data stays on your computer, you control retention. You can delete medication reminders, chat history, and debug logs at any time within the Sunny app. Uninstalling Sunny removes all local data associated with the app.

7. Security

Credentials you choose to store (such as optional API keys) are encrypted using your operating systems built-in encryption (via Electron safeStorage). Credentials are never transmitted to Sunny developers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at mt25mb.github.io/Sunny/privacy-policy.html.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on GitHub:
github.com/MT25MB/Sunny/issues
